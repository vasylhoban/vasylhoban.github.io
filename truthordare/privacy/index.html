Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026.

In short: Truth or Dare: Nothing to Hide does not collect your personal data, apart from emails you choose to send us. Everything you enter in the game stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

To make the game work, the app stores on your device:

  • player names;

  • your last game format, the modes you chose, and an unfinished game with its score;

  • settings — theme and vibration;

  • a note that you confirmed you are 18 or older;

  • which cards have already been shown, so they don't repeat, and your bonus-card progress;

  • internal counters — games completed, when we last showed the rating request or the subscription screen — so we don't show them too often.

The app never sends this data to us or to third parties. If iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) is on, it is included in your own backup, which we cannot access. Deleting the app erases it from the phone.

Purchases and subscription

Subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only receives the purchase details it needs to unlock access: which plan is active, its dates, and the storefront country. We never see your card number, your name, or your Apple Account. You can manage your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs in the app. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell any data. You don't need an account, and we don't run any servers, so there's nowhere for your data to go.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports: device model, iOS and app version, and a technical trace of where the crash happened. They contain no name, contact details, or player names. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers.

If you email us

When you email our support address, we receive your address, your message, and anything you attach. We use them only to reply — based on your request and our legitimate interest in answering it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), (f)). We keep correspondence for no longer than 12 months after the last message, and delete it sooner on request.

Our support email runs on Gmail: Google stores messages as the email provider under its own privacy policy, including on servers outside Ukraine and the EU (for example, in the US), with the international-transfer safeguards Google provides. Apart from Google, we share your emails with no one. Please don't include intimate details about yourself or others.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no analytics or cookies here, but GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under its own privacy statement. We have no access to these logs.

Age

Truth or Dare: Nothing to Hide is a game for adults, 18+. It is not intended for minors, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a minor has emailed us, we will delete the message.

Age check by Apple. Where the law requires it (for example, in Texas, USA), the app asks Apple to confirm your age range — only whether you are 18 or older. Apple shares only the range and how it was confirmed (self-declared, ID, or payment card), never your date of birth; the app doesn't store it or send it anywhere. If the range is under 18, or you choose not to share it, the game won't open.

Your rights

Game data stays only on your phone, and we have no access to it: to delete it, delete the app. For emails you sent us, you can ask whether we hold them and get a copy; have them corrected or deleted; restrict or object to their processing; and receive them in a portable format. Write to the address below — we reply within 30 days.

You may also complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (ombudsman.gov.ua), your EU or EEA data protection authority, the UK ICO (ico.org.uk), or a court.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever start collecting data, we'll let you know in the app before we do.
