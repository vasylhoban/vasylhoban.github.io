Questions, ideas, or a card that felt off — write to appsvhoban@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. If it's about a specific card, please paste its text.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The quickest way is in the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Truth or Dare: Nothing to Hide → Cancel Subscription.

The subscription renews automatically. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Subscribed, but levels are still locked?

In the app, open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases (the button is also on the subscription screen). Make sure your phone is signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: reportaproblem.apple.com.

What's free, and what comes with a subscription?

Free: the first level of every mode — Warm-up in Butterflies and Couples, Icebreaker in With friends — 100 cards each. A Week or Year subscription unlocks everything: the Flirt and Hot levels for couples, the Party and Extreme modes for groups, plus cards added in future updates. Prices for your country are shown in the app.

Are the cards rules I have to follow?

No. Every truth and dare is just a suggestion. Players decide for themselves what to do and are responsible for their own choices. Anyone can skip any card, no explanation needed, and skipping carries no penalty.

Will my subscription move to a new phone?

Yes: it works on all your iPhones signed in with the same Apple Account — tap Restore purchases if needed. Player names and an unfinished game are stored only on the phone you played on and don't transfer — unless you restore the new phone from a backup.

Why are cards repeating?

Cards don't repeat until you've played all the cards available for the modes and levels you chose. After that, they cycle again. To get more new cards, add levels or modes — a subscription opens many more.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game stores lives on your phone. Delete the app, and the data goes with it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I share my subscription with my family?

No: Family Sharing is turned off for the subscription because the game is 18+ and a family group may include children. Your subscription works on all your iPhones signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Open the link with the code on your iPhone — the App Store will offer to redeem it. Or, in the App Store, tap your photo at the top → Redeem Gift Card or Code, and enter the code. What the code gives you and for how long is shown in the offer itself.

Which devices does the game run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On iPad, the game opens as an iPhone app. There's no Android version yet.
